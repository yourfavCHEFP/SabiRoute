### §9.2 The exact hash-input treatment — `entry_sha256`

**Owner decision — WP2-D2 requirement 6.** The inserted table must explicitly state that `entry_sha256` is **computed and excluded from the hash input**.

**The defect this prevents.** Read as a plain field table, the twelve keys invite an implementer to serialise all twelve and hash the object — **the hash then covers itself.** No digest can satisfy that, and no two implementations could agree on what to exclude. E2 would install a self-referential rule at the moment it replaces the HTML comment.

**Normative evidence (source).** The only fully specified, reproducible record is at `:314-329` and `:340-354`:

> `:314` `**Canonical genesis line** (508 bytes, no trailing LF in the hashed preimage):`
> `:328-329` `Key order produced by code-point sort: addendum_sha256, created, entry_type, ledger_id, owner_key_fingerprint, prev, protocol_sha256, seq, t16_sha256.`

`entry_sha256` is **absent** from that key order. `:340-354` builds a 9-key dict and hashes it; the digest is **produced, never inserted**.

**Measured confirmation — this session.** The genesis preimage at `:317` was hashed directly (`sed -n '317p' | head -c 508 | shasum -a 256`):

- **9 keys** present, `entry_sha256` absent ✅
- **508 bytes** without LF → `ad1484b0758f0accfd9986ed6f2d5f8fac1ded3a83d95974ed58af7fc74caf28` ✅ matches `:324`
- **509 bytes** with LF → `452a05bb8c241e46b3e86851821dd3daa604e478baad6ed41354e0dda592bbbe` ✅ matches `:325`

**The arithmetic, to be stated in the inserted table:**

| Record | Hashed (serialised) keys | Computed | Total ledger fields |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Genesis | 6 payload + 3 envelope = **9** | `entry_sha256` | 10 |
| VER entry | 8 payload + 3 envelope = **11** | `entry_sha256` | **12** |

**Rule to be stated:** `entry_sha256` is **computed, never serialised**. A VER entry's hashed object contains **eleven** keys — `seq`, `prev`, `entry_type` and the eight payload fields. The twelve-key count includes the computed digest as a ledger field.

This clarification **changes no schema byte** and is folded into E2 under WP2-D2 rather than raised as a new decision.

**Limitation, stated.** The reference generator at `:340-354` was **not executed**. What is established is the byte length, key membership, key order and both digests of the stored line. What is **not** established is that the nine stated inputs at `:298-312` regenerate that exact line. That residual gap is named, not filled.
