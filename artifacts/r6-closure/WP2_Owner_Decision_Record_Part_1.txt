## §4 Recording conventions

Three registers are used throughout and must not be conflated:

**Verbatim source text** is quoted in blockquotes and attributed by path and line. Source wording is never paraphrased into a requirement.

**Formal recording of an owner decision** is stated as a decision, in the owner's terms, and marked as such. It carries the authority of §1 and nothing more.

**Reconciliation findings** — verified anchors, measured digests, numbering maps and defect reports — are stated as measured facts with their location. They support the record; they are not decisions and acquire no authority from appearing in it.

Two conventions are load-bearing:

- **O-identifiers are namespace-qualified.** `N1-O1…N1-O5` are the WP-2 protocol parameters (S9 `:79-85`). `N2-O1…N2-O5` are the CLOSE-FINAL-03 closure blockers (S7 `:306-310`), which reassign the same labels. Unqualified `O5` is ambiguous across the corpus and is not used.
- **E-identifiers follow Scheme B** — the governing numbering (S8 `:500-506`), in which the five §4.5 corrections are E1, E2, E3, E5 and E6, E4 is the REG/VER asymmetry note, and E7 is the addendum's embedded reference. The earlier Scheme A labelling (E1–E5 sequential) is superseded by the owner's decision on D2.
