## §14 Deferred — the sixth O5 correction

**Owner instruction, verbatim:**

> Keep the sixth O5 correction deferred.

### §14.1 What the sixth correction is

The five mandatory O5 corrections are E1–E3 and E5–E6 (Part 2 §6). The **sixth** is a different question, and it is about the **token**, not the container:

> **The optional sixth.** The pack explicitly did **not** correct the `VER:v1` **token** itself. It appears in §6 and `PARAM-A.4` as a grammar name; retaining it as the *field-name set identifier* (while the container changes) is the least invasive option and keeps `PARAM-A.4` intact. The pack states: *"If the owner prefers to retire the token entirely, that is a sixth correction and a larger edit."*
> — `artifacts/r6-closure/wp2-decision-clarification-addendum-2026-11-10.md:311-315`

So the deferred question is: **retire the `VER:v1` token entirely, or retain it as the field-name-set identifier while the container changes from an HTML comment to a JSONL line?**

### §14.2 The decision: deferred, not dropped

**The sixth correction is DEFERRED.** It is not approved, not rejected, and not silently resolved by any other decision in this record.

**What deferral means concretely.** The `VER:v1` token is **retained** in protocol §6 and in `PARAM-A.4`. E2 replaces the *fenced HTML-comment block* with the 12-key table and **retains the six field names**; it does not retire the token. `PARAM-A.4` therefore survives E2 unamended, which is the outcome F-WP2-2 requires.

**What deferral does not mean.** It does not mean the token is now permanent. It means the question is open and must be asked again before any act that would make it expensive to answer — specifically before a real `VER` entry is written. A deferred question that is never re-asked becomes a silent decision, and that is the failure this section exists to prevent.

### §14.3 Why it was deferred rather than decided

Retiring the token is a **larger edit** than the five corrections: it touches the grammar name in §6, the reference in `PARAM-A.4`, and any text that names the record by token. It also has no effect on the bytes of any entry, because the token is a grammar identifier and not a field. Deferring it costs nothing today and avoids coupling a cosmetic rename to a byte-moving pass.

**Recorded status: `VER:v1` token — RETAINED, question OPEN, correction DEFERRED.**
