# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

SabiRoute is a technical project where people should be able to participate without harassment or intimidation. We aim to make project discussions welcoming, patient, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect, including when disagreeing about technical choices.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking the person who proposed them.
- Keep feedback specific, useful, and proportionate.
- Respect privacy; do not publish another person's private information or private correspondence.
- Follow project maintainers' instructions in project spaces.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, sexualized attention without consent, and sharing private information without permission are not acceptable. Repeated hostile or demeaning conduct is also unacceptable, even when framed as humor or technical criticism.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in SabiRoute repositories, issue and pull-request discussions, project channels, and project-related events. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or exclude a participant when needed to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate to the conduct and consider the safety of affected people.

To report a concern, contact the maintainer privately through the [project owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/yourfavCHEFP). Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical; the project does not promise a particular response time.

## Questions

For questions about this policy, contact the project maintainer through the same private channel.
